Stillpoint — Room Difficulty Calculator

Pick a room. Runs a batch of random weighted-walk playthroughs (same engine family as floor_plan_simulation.html) from Spawn to that room, rolling the "keep the Child vs. Void Tether" choice per run, and averages what a player is actually carrying (abilities, Fracture Pips, Lore Pips, max health) the first time they arrive. Use that to gut-check how hard the room should hit back. Regenerate this file (node Plans/room_difficulty_calculator.js --html) after any floor_plan.md graph change.
