Dissect this entity's attacks

Only shows the attacks/states that belong to whichever entity is picked above — no more digging through the Sovereign's list to find a regular enemy's, or vice versa. Auto-generates a real frame-by-frame breakdown from that attack's actual current in-game numbers (hitbox, damage, knockback, timing) as a faithful starting point — a 🎬 marker means it's a real dissection (numerically identical to what's on screen today), a + marker means it's just a blank starting frame (state has no combat data to pull from, e.g. idle/walk). Overwrites existing key(s) if already present (asks first). Replace any frame's look with your own PNG (upload below) and drag its hitbox to match; everything else keeps working exactly as the game does today until you do.
