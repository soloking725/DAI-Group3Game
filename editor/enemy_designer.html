Defense (optional)

Regen (optional)

Heals over time unless it's taken damage recently (Timeworn Husk, expansion.md #11) — any hit resets the countdown.

Shield (optional)

A regenerating HP pool separate from Health, blocking melee hits only from the shielded (facing) side — ranged always pierces straight through to Health (Crystal Sentinel/Sovereign's Guard).

Boss Mode (optional)
