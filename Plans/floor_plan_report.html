Mandatory rooms

Removing this room alone (any incoming/outgoing door) makes the Final Boss unreachable. This is a stronger test than "is it on the shortest path" — it proves no alternate route exists.

Soft-lock risk scan

Checks every one-way / scripted-teleport connection: can the far side still reach the Final Boss? DEFINITE TRAP = unreachable even with every ability in the game, a real bug. DEPENDS ON LOADOUT = fine in the best case, worth a manual check for an underleveled player.

How many ways to beat the game?

Counts distinct room/ability-pickup sequences from Spawn to the Final Boss, including optional detours and backtracks — not just the mandatory skeleton. A huge number mostly reflects how many optional side-rooms and orderings exist, not "meaningfully different strategies"; the mandatory-rooms list above is the more useful measure of the game's actual shape.
